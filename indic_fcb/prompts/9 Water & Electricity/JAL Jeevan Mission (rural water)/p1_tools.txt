You are building a function-calling benchmark dataset in the style
of the Berkeley Function Calling Leaderboard (BFCL).

Your task: Generate a JSON array of tool schemas for the
Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) rural water supply service
on the UMANG app.

The number of tools should be sufficient (typically 12–16 tools)
to cover all realistic citizen interactions and edge cases.

---

## CORE OBJECTIVE

Design tools that enable realistic workflows such as:
- Citizen authentication (optional for personalized services)
- Checking household water connection status (FHTC)
- Applying for new tap connection
- Tracking application status
- Checking water supply availability (daily/periodic)
- Monitoring water quality
- Reporting water issues
- Tracking complaints and resolution
- Village-level water infrastructure insights

---

## CRITICAL DOMAIN CONSTRAINTS (MUST BE ENCODED IN DESCRIPTIONS)

- Public vs authenticated access:
  - Water supply status and general info are PUBLIC
  - Application, household-specific data, and complaints require authentication

- Household dependency:
  Services are tied to:
    - household ID
    - village / habitation

- FHTC (Functional Household Tap Connection):
  Connection must be approved and installed before service usage.

- Infrastructure dependency:
  Water supply depends on:
    - village infrastructure
    - pipelines and storage systems

- Supply variability:
  Water supply may vary by:
    - time of day
    - availability
    - infrastructure conditions

- Water quality monitoring:
  Quality parameters may include:
    - potability
    - contamination indicators

- Complaint dependency:
  Issues must be reported before escalation.

- No guaranteed continuous supply:
  System reflects real-world variability.

- No direct financial transfer:
  This is a service delivery system, not a subsidy.

---

## STATE TRANSITION REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

Tool descriptions MUST encode valid state transitions:

- connection_status:
  not_applied → applied → approved → installed → active → inactive

- supply_status:
  unavailable → intermittent → available

- quality_status:
  unknown → safe → unsafe → under_testing

- complaint_status:
  not_raised → raised → in_progress → resolved → rejected

- infrastructure_status:
  not_available → under_construction → operational → maintenance

Tools must define:
- when actions are valid
- when actions are invalid

---

## EDGE CASE REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

The toolset MUST support scenarios where:
- household not registered
- no infrastructure in village
- connection applied but not installed
- water supply intermittent or unavailable
- unsafe or contaminated water
- duplicate connection request
- incorrect household mapping
- complaint raised without issue verification
- delayed complaint resolution
- mismatch between reported and actual supply

Descriptions must explicitly reflect these cases.

---

## ERROR HANDLING REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

Each tool description MUST include:
- validation rules (household ID, village mapping)
- rejection conditions (invalid request, duplicate application)
- failure scenarios (infrastructure delay, supply issues)

Tools must NOT assume success-only execution.

---

## SCENARIO COVERAGE REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

The toolset MUST support:
- checking water connection status
- applying for new connection
- tracking supply and quality
- reporting issues
- tracking complaint resolution
- handling infrastructure-related delays
- grievance escalation
