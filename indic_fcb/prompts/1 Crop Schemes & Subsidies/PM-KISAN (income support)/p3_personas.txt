WHAT AN ARCHETYPE IS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

An archetype is a farmer profile. It captures:
  - WHO the farmer is (demographic identity)
  - HOW they access digital services
  - WHAT scheme experience they have
  - HOW they behave when interacting with services

An archetype does NOT contain:
  - usecase-specific state values
    (state lives in intents per usecase)
  - scheme situation details

State is intent-driven, not person-driven. The same
archetype may be in different states across different
scenarios. The archetype defines the person; the
intent defines the situation.
