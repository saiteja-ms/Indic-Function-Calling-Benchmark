You are building a function-calling benchmark dataset in the style
of the Berkeley Function Calling Leaderboard (BFCL).

Your task: Generate a JSON array of tool schemas for the
PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi - income support)
service on the UMANG app.

The number of tools should be sufficient (typically 12–16 tools)
to cover all realistic farmer interactions and edge cases.

---

## CORE OBJECTIVE

Design tools that enable realistic workflows such as:
- Farmer authentication
- Farmer registration and enrollment
- Eligibility verification
- eKYC completion
- Beneficiary status tracking
- Installment payment tracking
- Payment failure resolution
- Bank account verification/update
- Land record linkage validation
- Grievance handling

---

## CRITICAL DOMAIN CONSTRAINTS (MUST BE ENCODED IN DESCRIPTIONS)

- Authentication is REQUIRED:
  Aadhaar + OTP verification must be completed.
  Authentication returns a session_token.

- Eligibility dependency:
  Farmer must satisfy:
    - valid Aadhaar
    - valid land ownership records
    - not excluded under scheme rules

- Mandatory eKYC:
  eKYC must be completed before receiving payments.

- Installment-based payment:
  ₹6000/year is paid in 3 installments of ₹2000.

- Land record linkage:
  Land ownership must be verified via state databases.

- Bank account dependency:
  Payment is transferred directly to linked bank account.

- Payment dependency:
  Installment is credited only if:
    - eligibility confirmed
    - eKYC completed
    - bank details valid

- Duplicate prevention:
  One Aadhaar can have only one beneficiary record.

- Payment failure:
  Payments may fail due to incorrect bank details or mismatch.

- Grievances should ONLY be raised after checking payment or eligibility status.

---

## STATE TRANSITION REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

Tool descriptions MUST encode valid state transitions:

- registration_status:
  not_registered → registered → verified

- ekyc_status:
  not_started → pending → completed → failed

- eligibility_status:
  unknown → eligible → ineligible → rejected

- payment_status:
  not_initiated → pending → credited → failed

- installment_status:
  due → processed → credited → failed

Tools must define:
- when actions are valid
- when actions are invalid

---

## EDGE CASE REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

The toolset MUST support scenarios where:
- duplicate registration attempt
- invalid Aadhaar or mismatch
- land record not found or mismatched
- eKYC not completed but payment expected
- incorrect or inactive bank account
- payment failure due to mismatch
- ineligible farmer attempting registration
- delayed installment
- partial payment history
- incorrect beneficiary details

Descriptions must explicitly reflect these cases.
