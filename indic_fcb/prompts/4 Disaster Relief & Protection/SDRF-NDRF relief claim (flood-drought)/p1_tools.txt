You are building a function-calling benchmark dataset in the style
of the Berkeley Function Calling Leaderboard (BFCL).

Your task: Generate a JSON array of tool schemas for the
SDRF/NDRF (State Disaster Response Fund / National Disaster Response Fund)
relief claim service on the UMANG app.

The number of tools should be sufficient (typically 12–16 tools)
to cover all realistic farmer interactions and edge cases.

---

## CORE OBJECTIVE

Design tools that enable realistic workflows such as:
- Farmer authentication
- Reporting disaster-related crop damage
- Checking disaster declaration status
- Submitting relief claim applications
- Uploading damage evidence (structured inputs)
- Verifying location eligibility
- Survey and damage assessment tracking
- Relief approval/rejection
- Compensation calculation and tracking
- Grievance handling

---

## CRITICAL DOMAIN CONSTRAINTS (MUST BE ENCODED IN DESCRIPTIONS)

- Authentication is REQUIRED:
  Aadhaar + OTP verification must be completed.
  Authentication returns a session_token.

- Disaster declaration dependency:
  Relief claims are valid ONLY if:
    - disaster (flood/drought/etc.) is officially declared
    - location falls within affected area

- Location specificity:
  Claims must be tied to:
    - village/district
    - land parcel

- Relief vs insurance:
  SDRF/NDRF relief is separate from insurance (PMFBY).
  Duplicate compensation for same loss may be restricted.

- Time-bound reporting:
  Damage must be reported within a specified window after the disaster.

- Evidence requirement:
  Claim must include structured damage details (simulated inputs).

- Survey dependency:
  Government or local authority verification is required.

- Severity-based compensation:
  Relief depends on:
    - extent of damage
    - type of disaster

- Payment dependency:
  Relief is disbursed only after approval.

- Grievances should ONLY be raised after checking claim status.

---

## STATE TRANSITION REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

Tool descriptions MUST encode valid state transitions:

- claim_status:
  not_reported → reported → submitted → under_review → approved → rejected → compensated

- disaster_status:
  not_declared → declared → active → closed

- survey_status:
  not_started → scheduled → in_progress → completed

- payment_status:
  not_initiated → pending → credited → failed

Tools must define:
- when actions are valid
- when actions are invalid

---

## EDGE CASE REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

The toolset MUST support scenarios where:
- disaster not declared but claim attempted
- location not in affected zone
- reporting outside allowed time window
- incorrect or missing land/location details
- duplicate relief claim
- insufficient or conflicting damage information
- survey delay or missing verification
- claim rejected due to low damage severity
- overlapping insurance claim (PMFBY conflict)
- payment delay or failure

Descriptions must explicitly reflect these cases.

---

## ERROR HANDLING REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

Each tool description MUST include:
- validation rules (disaster declaration, location eligibility)
- rejection conditions (invalid claim, ineligible region)
- failure scenarios (survey delay, payment failure)

Tools must NOT assume success-only execution.
