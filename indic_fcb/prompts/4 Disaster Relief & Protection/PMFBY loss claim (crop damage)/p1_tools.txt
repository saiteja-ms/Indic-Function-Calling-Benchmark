You are building a function-calling benchmark dataset in the style
of the Berkeley Function Calling Leaderboard (BFCL).

Your task: Generate a JSON array of tool schemas for the
PMFBY Loss Claim (crop damage reporting and compensation claim)
service on the UMANG app.

The number of tools should be sufficient (typically 12–16 tools)
to cover all realistic farmer interactions and edge cases.

---

## CORE OBJECTIVE

Design tools that enable realistic workflows such as:
- Farmer authentication
- Reporting crop damage events
- Verifying policy linkage
- Submitting loss claims
- Uploading evidence (structured inputs)
- Survey and field inspection tracking
- Loss assessment and validation
- Claim approval/rejection
- Compensation calculation
- Payment tracking
- Grievance handling

---

## CRITICAL DOMAIN CONSTRAINTS (MUST BE ENCODED IN DESCRIPTIONS)

- Authentication is REQUIRED:
  Aadhaar + OTP verification must be completed.
  Authentication returns a session_token.

- Policy dependency:
  Claim can ONLY be filed if:
    - farmer has an active PMFBY policy
    - crop and land parcel match the insured details

- Time-bound reporting:
  Crop loss must be reported within a fixed time window
  after the event (e.g., within 72 hours).

- Loss types:
  Claims may be triggered by:
    - drought
    - flood
    - pest attack
    - cyclone
    - unseasonal rainfall

- Evidence requirement:
  Claim must include:
    - structured loss details
    - optional supporting evidence (simulated)

- Survey dependency:
  Field survey or remote sensing is required
  before claim approval.

- Partial vs full loss:
  Compensation depends on severity of loss.

- Duplicate claims:
  Multiple claims for the same event/land are NOT allowed.

- Payment dependency:
  Compensation is processed ONLY after claim approval.

- Grievances should ONLY be raised after checking claim status.

---

## STATE TRANSITION REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

Tool descriptions MUST encode valid state transitions:

- claim_status:
  not_reported → reported → submitted → under_review → approved → rejected → compensated

- survey_status:
  not_started → scheduled → in_progress → completed

- assessment_status:
  not_assessed → partially_assessed → fully_assessed

- payment_status:
  not_initiated → pending → credited → failed

Tools must define:
- when actions are valid
- when actions are invalid

---

## EDGE CASE REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

The toolset MUST support scenarios where:
- claim filed without active policy
- reporting after allowed time window
- invalid or unsupported loss type
- incorrect land or crop mapping
- duplicate claim submission
- insufficient evidence
- survey delay or missing survey
- conflicting survey results
- claim rejected due to low damage threshold
- partial compensation disputes
- payment failure or delay

Descriptions must explicitly reflect these cases.

---

## ERROR HANDLING REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

Each tool description MUST include:
- validation rules (policy active, time window, land linkage)
- rejection conditions (invalid claim, duplicate claim)
- failure scenarios (survey delay, claim rejection, payment failure)

Tools must NOT assume success-only execution.

---

## SCENARIO COVERAGE REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)

The toolset MUST support:
- farmer reporting crop loss
- submitting valid claim
- tracking claim lifecycle
- survey and assessment process
- claim approval and compensation
- claim rejection scenarios
- grievance escalation
